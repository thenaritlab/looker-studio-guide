## 👋 What is this guide?

A hands-on course for **Google Looker Studio**, built for complete beginners — no dashboard experience needed. We take you step by step, from opening the tool for the first time to publishing a 3-page business dashboard you built yourself.

| | What you get |
|---|---|
| 📖 | **16 chapters** ordered from easy to advanced, ~1 hour each |
| 🧪 | **13 hands-on labs** with expected results at every step |
| 📦 | **6 sample datasets** (sales, customers, products, marketing, web, HR) — fully synthetic, safe to practice on |
| 🗓️ | **6-week study plan**, 1 hour a day — start any day, no calendar dates |
| 🏆 | **Capstone** — a 3-page dashboard ready for your portfolio |

> [!NOTE]
> Every chapter exists in two languages — [English](docs/en/00-toc.md) and [Thai](docs/th/00-toc.md) — with a switcher on every page.

## 🙋 Who is it for?

- Office workers who build reports in Excel and want to level up to dashboards
- Marketers and business owners who want their numbers to update themselves
- Students and career changers building their first data portfolio piece
- Tableau / Power BI users adding another tool to their belt

## 🎒 What you need

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| ✅ Google account | The free one you already use every day |
| ✅ ~1 hour a day | 5 days a week, for 6 weeks |
| ⬜ BigQuery sandbox | Used from the middle chapters — free, **no credit card** (set up in chapter 3) |

> [!TIP]
> Nothing to install — Looker Studio runs entirely in your browser.

## 🚀 How to start (4 steps)

1. 📖 Read the [table of contents & how-to](docs/en/00-toc.md) — 15 minutes
2. 🗓️ Open the [6-week roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and begin **Day 1**
3. 📦 In [Lab 03](labs/lab03-data-sources/README.md) you'll load the [sample datasets](datasets/README.md) into Google Sheets / BigQuery
4. 🏆 Finish with the [Capstone](docs/en/14-capstone.md) and publish it via [chapter 99](docs/en/99-publish-to-github.md)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Falling behind? **Skip a Stretch goal — never skip a lab.** Skills come from doing.

## 📦 Sample datasets

Synthetic data from an imaginary retail company, covering problems you'll meet in real work:

| File | About | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| [sales_orders](datasets/sales_orders.csv) | ~19,600 order lines | Sales, profit, trends |
| [customers](datasets/customers.csv) | Customer master | Segments, regions |
| [products](datasets/products.csv) | Product master | Categories, brands |
| [marketing_campaigns](datasets/marketing_campaigns.csv) | Ad campaigns | ROI, funnels |
| [web_traffic](datasets/web_traffic.csv) | Website traffic | Sessions, conversions |
| [hr_headcount](datasets/hr_headcount.csv) | HR (deliberately messy) | Data cleaning |

Every column is documented in the [data dictionary](datasets/README.md).
